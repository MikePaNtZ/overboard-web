1 · How the live one movesPitches about its own wheel axis, deck stays aligned to the gold line.

The wobble, rebuilt.

Previously it rotated the whole character like a metronome, which read as “swinging” rather than “balancing.” Now the pivot is the wheel centre and the motion is a canned repeating trajectory: small hunting corrections, then a knock that noses it down, then a decaying recovery — which is exactly the impulse-response test the sim already runs.

Motion 1 — recommended

Small hunting, occasional knock

Idle ±1.4°, one −7° nose-down per cycle, settles in about three oscillations.
